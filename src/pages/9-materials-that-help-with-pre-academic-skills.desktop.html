In Plain Language
When your child cannot yet distinguish "b" from "d," this is not carelessness — it is the visual cortex's orientation-detection network still calibrating. When your child loses track counting beyond 6, it is the prefrontal working memory system still expanding its hold capacity.
These are developmental processes, not deficits. They respond to specific, targeted input — exactly what the 9 materials on this page provide.
